# Market, Exchange & Liquidity Infrastructure

> A code-free case study of exchange and liquidity systems I have helped lead and build as Co-founder and CTO of Aryze.

## What it enables

This work brings together the components needed to quote, route, execute, inspect, and present digital-asset exchange activity. The focus is the whole product boundary: not only market logic, but also APIs, indexing, interfaces, data quality, operational visibility, and integration tooling.

Representative capability areas include:

- Swap and liquidity interfaces
- Routing and smart-order selection
- SDKs and reusable market primitives
- Limit-order relay and indexing services
- Market, pool, and token data presentation
- Subgraphs and event-derived data products
- Trading and treasury automation experiments

## The engineering problem

Market systems connect multiple sources of state that can change independently. Users need a coherent view of price, route, liquidity, approval, execution, and final status, while operators need enough evidence to diagnose discrepancies and keep integrations reliable.

## My contribution

My contribution has spanned technical direction, architecture, integration strategy, hands-on engineering, product interfaces, data and indexing workflows, infrastructure, and the evaluation and adaptation of upstream open-source protocols.

Where the work builds on open-source exchanges, routers, SDKs, or interface projects, authorship remains with the upstream maintainers. Aryze-specific integration and product work remains company work.

## Technology areas

Solidity, TypeScript, exchange protocols, routing, indexing, subgraphs, APIs, real-time data, web applications, automation, and cloud infrastructure.

## Source and confidentiality

This repository is intentionally documentation-only. It contains no private Aryze source code, trading credentials, proprietary routing logic, private endpoints, customer data, or internal operational controls. The underlying company work remains private and owned by Aryze.
